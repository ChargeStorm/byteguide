\section{Energy Meter \& status reports using MQTT}
\label{sec:energy-meter-status-mqtt}

This was added in the CCU FW r3.11.15.1 and r3.12.6.

\subsection{Configuration}
\label{sec:em-status-configuration}

The following configuration keys can be set:
\begin{description}
\item[Automation/MqttEnabled] Boolean, set to \verb|true| to enable the service.
\item[Automation/MqttServer] The address / IP address of the MQTT broker. An
  empty string will use the internal MQTT broker on the Charging Station
  itself.
\item[Automation/MqttLogin] Login / username for the MQTT broker, if
  necessary. Otherwise, leave empty.
\item[Automation/MqttPassword] Password for the MQTT broker, if
  necessary. Otherwise, leave empty.
\item[Automation/MqttBaseTopic] Base topic to be use. If empty, \verb|CTEK|
  will be used.
\end{description}

The topics described below, assumes that \verb|[Automation/MqttBaseTopic| is
empty, thus they're starting with \verb|CTEK|. Thereafter, the identity of the
charging station comes, denoted by \verb|CBID| in the topics.

\subsection{Energy Meter data}
\label{sec:em-mqtt}

The following topics includet energy meter readings, according to the JSON
structure below:
\begin{description}
\item[CTEK/CBID/mainMeter/em]
\item[CTEK/CBID/evse1/em]
\item[CTEK/CBID/evse2/em]
\end{description}

\begin{lstlisting}[language=json,caption={Energy meter readings.}]
    {
        "current":[1.5889999866485596,0.8399999737739563,3.7209999561309814],
        "energy":20218145,
        "frequency": 50,
        "power":1337.5999755859375,
        "timestamp":"2021-04-09T09:09:47Z",
        "voltage":[237.60000610351562,233.89999389648438,235.80000305175781]
    } 
\end{lstlisting}

The data are taken from the energy meter and should, apart from possible
rounding errors correspond to the meter readings.

Some fields can be missing, if the energy meter doesn't support them, or the
data can be reported as \verb|nan| or \verb|null|.

\subsection{Energy meter information}
\label{sec:energy-meter-inform}

The following topics includes general information about the energy meter:
\begin{description}
\item[CTEK/CBID/mainMeter/meterinfo] Currently not being sent, but will likely
  be added in the future.
\item[CTEK/CBID/evse1/meterinfo]
\item[CTEK/CBID/evse2/meterinfo] 
\end{description}

\begin{lstlisting}[language=json,caption={General information from the energy meter.}]
{
  "serialno": "xxxxxxx"
}
\end{lstlisting}


\subsection{EVSE status information}
\label{sec:evse-stat-inform}


The following topics has status informations from the EVSE(s):
\begin{description}
\item[CTEK/CBID/evse1/status]
\item[CTEK/CBID/evse2/status]
\end{description}

\begin{lstlisting}[language=json,caption={EVSE information and status.}]
{
    "assigned":16,
    "state":"NCRQ",
    "timestamp":"2021-04-09T09:09:47Z"
}
\end{lstlisting}

Assigned is the current assigned to the EV by the charging station.

The state is a four letter code:
\begin{description}
\item[AVAL] Available, no EV connected.
\item[PAUS] Pause, the charging station doesn't allow charging.
\item[EVRD] EV is ready.
\item[CHRG] Charging is on-going.
\item[FLTY] Faulty, something is wrong.
\item[DSBL] Disabled.
\item[CONN] EV is connected, waiting for authentication.
\item[NCRQ] No charging is requested by EV.
\item[AUTH] Authenticated, waiting for EV to be connected.
\item[INVL] Invalid, something is wrong.
\item[GONE] The EV has disappeared.
\item[DONE] The charging session is finished.
\item[SUHT] Temporary state while going to pause.
\item[STHT] Temporary state while stopping the charging session.
\end{description}


\section{Modbus/TCP for the internal Energy Meters}
\label{sec:modb-intern-energy}


This was added in the CCU FW r3.11.16 and r3.12.6.

This interface will expose the internal energy meters on a Modbus/TCP
interface. The primary purpose is to be able to read the energy register from
a system for individual metering and billing, ``Individuell Mätning och
Debitering'' in Swedish (abbreviated ``IMD'').

\subsection{Configuration}
\label{sec:modbus-configuration}

The following configuration key controls the service:
\begin{description}
\item[Automation/ModbusTCPEnable] Set this to \verb|true|, to enable the service.
\end{description}

Once the service is started, it will be available on the standard Modbus/TCP
port 502. The \verb|unitId| in the header frame is used to select between the
two EVSEs (outlets/cables). \verb|unitId| 1 is used for EVSE 1 (left outlet in
dual station, or the only outlet for a single station) and \verb|unitId| 2 for
EVSE 2, (right outlet for a dual station).

\subsection{Register map}
\label{sec:register-map}

The current register map is being used is shown it
table~\ref{tab:register-map}. Note that all registers shown are 16\,bits
wide.

\begin{table}[h!]
  \begin{center}
    \caption{Register map.}
    \label{tab:register-map}
    \begin{tabular}{r|l}
      \toprule
      \textbf{Register address} & \textbf{Contents} \\
      \midrule
      0x1000 & API Version \\
      0x1001 & API Status. 0 is OK. \\
      0x1002 & EnergyMeterType enum \\
      0x1003 & Char 1 and 2 of energy meter serial number \\
      0x1004 & Char 3 and 4 of energy meter serial number \\
      0x1005 & Char 5 and 6 of energy meter serial number \\
      0x1006 & Char 7 and 8 of energy meter serial number \\
      0x1007 & Char 9 and 10 of energy meter serial number \\
      0x1008 & Char 11 and 12 of energy meter serial number \\
      \midrule
      0x1100 & Total accumulated energy in Wh (high word - first 16 bits) \\
      0x1101 & Total accumulated energy in Wh (low word - last 16 bits) \\
      0x1102 & Current in L1. Unit: A*1000 \\
      0x1103 & Current in L2. Unit: A*1000 \\
      0x1104 & Current in L3. Unit: A*1000 \\
      0x1105 & Voltage, L1-N. Unit: V*10 \\
      0x1106 & Voltage, L2-N. Unit: V*10 \\
      0x1107 & Voltage, L3-N. Unit: V*10 \\
      0x1108 & Total power. Unit: W \\
      \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
  \end{center}
\end{table}
